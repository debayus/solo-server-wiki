    Monster Spawning

    SYS-15

    Summary

    Monsters spawn from spawn areas placed by hand on the map, following each zone's spawn table, day/night, and special boss rules.

    Rules

    Regular Spawn Areas

    • Each Spawn Area has an entries table (written in the zone catalog, SpawnAreaData): monster, weight, and spawn time (any / day / night).
    • When a zone is loaded, the area is filled right away up to max_alive.
    • An area keeps the number of living monsters at no more than max_alive. The respawn_sec timer starts when the living count is below max_alive and stops while the game is paused; each time the timer runs out the area spawns one new monster, rolled by weight from the entries that match the current time.
    • A monster spawns at a random point inside the area, off the player's screen, and walkable (not overlapping world collision). How to check both conditions: World and map. If there is no point off screen (the screen is wider than the area, for example at ×2 zoom), a random walkable point at least the fallback spawn distance from the player is used. If there is still no point that qualifies, the area tries again every retry interval (Formulas & Numbers).
    • The position where that monster spawned becomes its Spawn Point (Monster Behavior).
    • Monsters are not saved (Save system) and are discarded together with their zone when the zone is dropped from memory.
    • Monster levels follow the data; they do not adjust to the player's level.
    • When day turns to night (or the reverse), monsters that do not match the time stay alive until they die or leave the screen, and are then not spawned again.

    Boss

    • Each boss has a fixed arena in its zone, one boss per arena. An arena is a circle (the center and radius are written in the zone catalog, for example Zone 1 map); the player is "inside the arena" when the distance from the player to the arena center is ≤ the radius.
    • An arena entrance has no barrier: the player is free to enter and leave.
    • Boss availability: never defeated, or the current game time ≥ boss_defeated_at + boss_respawn_game_days (Player state). Availability is only checked when the player enters the arena, and when the zone is loaded if the player is already inside the arena. A boss does not suddenly appear while the player is already inside the arena.
    • The boss spawns at the center of the arena if it is available when checked; that point also becomes the Spawn Point for idle wandering (Monster Behavior). A boss that has already spawned stays (Idle, full HP) until its zone is dropped from memory; re-entering the arena does not create a new boss and does not emit boss_spawned again.
    • Boss music: the rules are in Music.
    • After being defeated, the boss disappears from the arena; it becomes available again after boss_respawn_game_days and spawns again following the availability rule above.
    • first_kill_drops are given only once per boss (recorded in boss_first_kills).
    • Every Z1–Z6 boss must have in first_kill_drops: the Palace Key Shard of its zone (Palace Key Shards) and Rebirth Crystal. In its regular drops, a Z1–Z6 boss also has Rebirth Crystal with a chance of 0.05.
    • Rebirth Crystal: regular drops are always rolled, including on the First Kill (the First Kill gives one guaranteed item from first_kill_drops, plus the drops roll); the night bonus applies (5%, or 6% at night; Formulas & Numbers). So its source is: 5% (6% at night) on every kill, apart from the guaranteed First Kill drop.
    • The player leaves the arena: the boss goes to the Return state toward the arena center (Monster Behavior): invulnerable, cannot be targeted, and releases its target; once it arrives, its HP fully recovers.
    • boss_fight_active (the boss is alive and the player is inside the arena) is defined in Autosave.

    Formulas & Numbers

    All numbers in this section.

    RuleDefault value
    max_alive per area4
    respawn_sec20 real sec
    Retry interval when there is no point off screen1 real sec
    Fallback spawn distance from the player (when there is no point off screen)192 px (12 tiles)
    boss_respawn_game_days1 game day (= 24 real minutes)

    The default values of max_alive and respawn_sec are used when a spawn block does not write other values. A zone catalog may override the defaults per spawn block (for example Zone 1 spawns).
