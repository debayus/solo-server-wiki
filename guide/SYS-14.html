    Gathering Materials

    SYS-14

    Summary

    Gather Nodes (mines, plants, trees) on the map give materials that do not come from monsters, then grow back after some time. Supports the Survival and Crafting pillars.

    Rules

    • Gather Nodes are placed by hand on the map of each zone. Their types and counts are written in the zone catalog (CNT-Z1 … CNT-Z7).
    • Gathering needs no tool.
    • The player taps a Gather Node → walks there → a progress bar runs for gather_time_sec → the result is rolled from yields and goes into the Bag.
    • A Gather Node is not a navigation obstacle, except a node with footprint_cells > 0 (GatherNodeData): that many tiles in the base row (centered on the base) have collision like a tree, and the collision edge of that node = the edge of its footprint square. The player stops as soon as they are within the Interact Distance (Formulas & Numbers); progress starts at that moment.
    • Stamina is checked when the player arrives (the start of progress) and deducted when progress finishes. If it is too low, gathering is refused with the toast sys.stamina_low ("Not enough stamina.").
    • If Stamina drops below the cost during progress (for example because of Hunger or an earlier sprint), gathering still finishes and Stamina is deducted down to a minimum of 0.
    • After being gathered, the Gather Node is Depleted until the regrowth time passes. Its appearance follows depleted_visual: hide = the sprite, shadow, and tap area are hidden until it recovers (it cannot be tapped), but its place still blocks placing stations (Rules) so a node that recovers is not overwritten; dim = stays in place at alpha 0.35 (the shadow follows that alpha; the footprint collision stays); stump = the sprite is replaced by the depleted_sprite asset (GatherNodeData, for example the stump obj_data_tree_stump) at full alpha until it recovers: an elliptical shadow at the base of the stump, raised by 0.35 × the ellipse height, the footprint collision shrinks to depleted_footprint_cells tiles at the base (default 1, GatherNodeData), the tap area = the stump sprite, and the stump does not take part in the see-through canopy (World and map). All tiles of the original footprint still block placing stations (Rules). If depleted_sprite is empty, stump behaves like dim.
    • A stump node that recovers returns to its full footprint; if the player or a monster is standing on a footprint tile that is about to have collision again, recovery is postponed to the next check (Regrowth) when that tile is empty, so nobody gets trapped inside collision.
    • Tapping a Depleted dim or stump Gather Node: the player does not walk, the toast sys.node_depleted ("Gather node has not recovered yet.") appears, and the target is released.
    • Rolling yields: each entry is rolled independently with the same amount rules as monster drops (Monster Death), without the night bonus (Formulas & Numbers). If every entry fails (an empty result), Stamina is still spent, the node is still Depleted, and the toast sys.gather_empty ("Nothing gathered.") appears.
    • The result always enters the Bag in full because the Bag is unlimited (Stacking).
    • Materials that enter the Bag are recorded in codex_items (Materials).
    • Progress is cancelled without spending Stamina if the player moves or is attacked. "Attacked" only means taking damage from a monster attack; Hunger 0 damage, Heat Zone damage, and lightning do not cancel it.
    • A menu opened during progress: progress is paused together with the game, then resumed when the menu closes.
    • Stamina does not recover during gathering progress.

    Regrowth

    • Regrowth is checked when a zone is loaded and every 1 game minute.
    • A node recovers when absolute_time ≥ depleted_at + respawn_game_min, where depleted_at = the value in depleted_nodes (Player state); then its entry is removed from depleted_nodes.

    Formulas & Numbers

    All numbers in this section.

    RuleDefault value
    gather_time_sec1.5 sec
    Stamina per gather5
    respawn_game_min120 game minutes (= 2 real minutes, Formulas & Numbers)
    Gather Node Interact Distance32 px, edge to edge of the collision shapes (player and Gather Node)
