    Controls

    SYS-10

    Summary

    Twin-thumb mobile controls: an analog joystick under the left thumb for movement, HUD buttons under the right thumb for Dodge and Attack. A quick tap still works on either half of the screen.

    Rules

    InputResult
    Tap the ground (anywhere)The character walks to that point (NavigationAgent2D pathfinding)
    Tap a monsterThe character moves in until within range (Player) and then attacks automatically until the target is released (Attack Flow); a tapped target aims at the body. A monster in the Return state cannot be targeted: the tap is ignored (the player does not move, and it is not treated as a ground tap)
    Tap a part button on the TargetFrame (while there is a target)The attack is aimed at that part (to break it); rules: Body Parts, display: HUD components
    Tap an object (campfire, anvil, gather node, gate)Walk to the object and then interact (Stations, Gathering Materials, Palace Key Shards)
    Approach an item on the groundPicked up automatically (Monster Death)
    Dodge buttonA short dash away from the target, using Stamina
    Attack buttonFind the nearest enemy within the TARGET_RADIUS_PX radius (center to center), make it the target, and then approach-and-attack (Attack Flow). No enemy in the radius = no-op. The attack range stays at 8 px edge to edge
    Hold a finger on the left half of the screenAnalog joystick: direction = the vector from the initial touch point to the finger's current position (screen coordinates); speed is proportional to the push distance. Lifting the finger stops analog movement
    Hold a finger on the right half of the screenDoes not move the character (HUD button zone)
    • The screen is split in two vertically; the boundary is at viewport_width / 2. A finger on a HUD button (mouse_filter = STOP) is consumed by the GUI before world input.
    • Desktop (for testing): left click = tap; left click held on the left half = analog; Space = Dodge.
    • Multi-touch is supported: the left finger drives the analog stick while the right finger presses HUD buttons, at the same time (twin-thumb).
    • A tap on a UI element must not be passed through to the world. A press that starts on a UI element never moves the character, even if the finger later slides into the world.

    Left Analog Joystick

    • Movement direction = current_screen − origin_screen (screen coordinates), stable even when the camera moves.
    • len = the length of that screen direction vector.
    • len ≤ DEAD_ZONE_PX → stand still.
    • DEAD_ZONE_PX < len < FULL_PUSH_PX → speed is linearly interpolated between walking speed (at the edge of the dead zone) and full running speed (at FULL_PUSH_PX).
    • len ≥ FULL_PUSH_PX → full running speed.
    • Movement goes through _move_sliding directly (not NavigationAgent2D).
    • Starting analog movement releases the attack target (consistent with target release in Attack Flow).
    • Stamina is consumed only when the target speed exceeds walking speed; when Stamina runs out, speed is capped at walking speed.
    • Analog mode lasts until the finger is lifted, even if the finger crosses into the right half. A second finger in the left zone while analog is active is ignored for analog.
    • After a Dodge: if the left finger is still pressed, analog movement resumes (twin-thumb).

    Dodge

    • Dash direction: away from the target if there is one; with no target = the current movement direction; if the character is standing still = the last facing direction (initially: down).
    • The dash lasts for the invulnerability duration (Formulas & Numbers) and stops if it hits a collision or reaches the edge of the walkable area.
    • Rejected (at no cost) if Stamina is below the Dodge cost or the character is already dashing. The distance, cost, and invulnerability duration are in Formulas & Numbers.
    • After the dash: if the left analog is still active, the character continues analog movement; if the target still exists and analog is not active, the character automatically moves in again and then attacks; otherwise it stands still.
    • Analog movement (including after a dash) releases the attack target, so the "continue analog" rule wins over "move in again".
