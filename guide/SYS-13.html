    Combat

    SYS-13

    Summary

    Automatic attacks on a target, the damage formula with DEF and element, monster stats from the level curve, breaking body parts, and XP from kills. Supports the Hunting pillar.

    Rules

    Attack Flow

    • The player attacks automatically while the target is within range (Player). Range is measured edge to edge of the collision shapes (Creatures), not center to center.
    • The first attack after the target enters range (for both player and monsters) is preceded by a wind-up. Damage is applied after waiting for the longer of the wind-up and the remaining attack interval, so moving in and out of range does not speed up attacks. Durations: Creatures.
    • The target is released when:
      • the target dies;
      • the player taps the ground;
      • the player uses the left analog joystick (Left Analog Joystick);
      • the player taps another monster (the target switches);
      • the player taps an object (Controls);
      • the player dies;
      • the target is in the Return state: because it passed its leash distance, reached the edge of its spawn area (territorial), or the player died; for a boss, because the player left the arena (Monster Behavior);
      • the target despawns (for example, its zone was dropped from memory, Regular Spawn Areas).
    • Dodging does not release the target: after the dash, if the target is still there, the player automatically moves in again and attacks (Dodge).
    • A monster in the Return state cannot be targeted: tapping it is ignored (the player does not move and the tap does not count as a ground tap).
    • The element of a player attack = the weapon's element (none without a weapon). The element of a monster attack = the monster's element.
    • Monsters cannot crit.
    • While dodging, the player is invulnerable for the invulnerability duration.

    Damage

    mitigation = 100 / (100 + target_DEF)
damage     = max(1, round(attacker_ATK × mitigation × element_multiplier × crit_multiplier))
    • element_multiplier (player → monster): the table in Formulas & Numbers.
    • Monster → player: element_multiplier is replaced by (1 − resist_X / 100), where X = the monster's element and the resist comes from Derived Stats. A monster without an element: ×1.0.
    • crit_multiplier = 1.5 on a crit (chance = the player's Crit), otherwise 1.0.
    • The night bonus is already included in monster ATK (Monster Stats), so there is no night multiplier in the damage formula.
    • round = rounding half away from zero, the same as Upgrade Table.

    Monster Stats

    Monsters do not store raw HP/ATK/DEF. The values are computed from the level and the multipliers in the monster data:

    HP  = round((100 + 12 × level) × hp_mult  × rank_hp_multiplier  × night_multiplier)
ATK = round((10 + 2.4 × level) × atk_mult × rank_atk_multiplier × night_multiplier)
DEF = round((2 × level)        × def_mult × rank_def_multiplier)

    night_multiplier = ×1.2 for HP and ATK if the monster spawns at night (Formulas & Numbers), otherwise ×1.0. The value is locked when the monster spawns; a monster that is already alive does not change when day and night switch. The HP from this formula is the max HP at spawn.

    Body Parts

    • The part to aim at is chosen with the part list button on the TargetFrame (HUD components); there is no sprite position data. A newly tapped target is aimed at the body (not a part). A part that is already broken is not shown and cannot be selected.
    • While the player aims at a body part, every point of damage to the monster also reduces that part's HP.
    • Part HP = round(max HP at spawn × hp_ratio).
    • When the part's HP runs out, the part breaks: bonus_drops are rolled immediately and fall to the ground, and the Codex entry is updated (Unlocking Monster Info). Exception: when fighting the final boss with game_cleared = false, the results go straight into the Bag as Unique Drops (Monster Death).
    • A broken part can no longer be aimed at; the aim returns to the body.
    • If a single hit both breaks a part and kills the monster, the part counts as broken first: bonus_drops still drop.
    • When the monster recovers (Monster Behavior), a part that is already broken stays broken (it gives no bonus_drops again); parts that are not yet broken recover to full HP as well.

    Monster Death

    • Drops are rolled from drops in the monster data (MonsterData) when the monster dies, and from bonus_drops when a part breaks. Each DropEntry is rolled independently with its chance multiplied by the night bonus (Formulas & Numbers, max 1.0).
    • A successful entry: the amount = a uniformly random integer amount_min..amount_max; it produces one Ground Item with that amount, placed at a random point within the Scatter Radius of the monster (Creatures).
    • Normal drops (drops, a body part's bonus_drops) fall to the ground near the monster as Ground Items.
    • Unique Drops go straight into the Bag: first_kill_drops, and every drop from the fight against the final boss while game_cleared = false, including the bonus_drops of parts broken in the middle of the fight (Ending); key items go into held_keys. The Bag is unlimited, so Unique Drops always enter in full (Stacking).
    • Ground Items are picked up automatically when the player is within the Pickup Radius and always enter the Bag in full (Stacking). The sys.bag_full toast has been removed.
    • Permanent Ground Items have no lifetime and are saved in persistent_ground_items (Player state); when picked up, the entry is removed. Their source is the Kingdom Relics (Zone 1 Kingdom Relics); Unique Drops no longer become permanent Ground Items because the Bag is never full.
    • Normal Ground Items disappear when their lifetime runs out and are not saved (Save system). The lifetime stops while the game is paused.
    • Normal Ground Items in a zone that is dropped from memory (Scene flow) disappear too; permanent Ground Items stay saved.
    • A Ground Item does not remember the monster it came from.
    • XP is given to the player; the Codex is updated (Discovery & Codex).

    Attack Button Flow

    When the player presses the Attack button (HUD components):

    1. Collect every monster in the Monster.GROUP group that can be targeted (is_targetable(); a monster in the Return state cannot be targeted).
    2. Pick the monster with the smallest center-to-center distance that is ≤ TARGET_RADIUS_PX (inclusive). If several monsters are at the same distance, pick the one with the smallest get_instance_id() (deterministic).
    3. If one is found: call set_target(monster) → the existing approach-and-attack logic handles the rest (8 px range, edge to edge).
    4. If no monster is within the radius: no-op (no effect, no new SFX).
    5. Rejected (no-op) while is_input_locked is active (Death Sequence), while dashing, or while Place Mode is active.

    TARGET_RADIUS_PX is a constant in scripts/systems/targeting.gd and is recorded in the Formulas & Numbers table above (the point where the document is read). This constant is not part of the 8 px attack range; after a target is picked, the attack still waits until the player is within 8 px edge to edge of the target.

    XP from Kills

    The constant 25 was chosen so a player goes from the starting level to the final level of a zone in about 70 kills of same-level monsters on average. With the starting XP table from Level & XP and a fixed addition of 10, the calculation (total XP to the next level ÷ XP of a same-level monster, from the starting level to the zone's final level) is: Z1 ±33 (Lv 1→2 takes just 2 kills), Z2 ±56, Z3 ±66, Z4 ±84, Z5 ±91, Z6 ±85, Z7 ±63. Z1 is deliberately faster so new players quickly feel what leveling up is like. Per-zone adjustments are evaluated during playtesting (Roadmap backlog).

    All numbers in this section.

    XP = round((25 × monster_level ^ 1.2 + 10) × rank_xp_multiplier × level_gap_multiplier)

    The two multipliers are multiplied together. The fixed addition of 10 is added before both multipliers: a boss is still exactly ×10 a same-level normal monster, and the level gap multiplier still cuts the whole XP so hunting monsters far below your level is not profitable. The effect is large at low levels (Lv 1: 25 → 35) and almost disappears at high levels (Lv 50: 2733 → 2743).
