    Upgrade & Evolution

    SYS-06

    Summary

    Weapons and armor are raised from +0 to +5; at +5 they can evolve into a new item.

    Rules

    • An upgrade always succeeds (there is no chance of failure).
    • "Same-rarity material" / "higher-rarity material" = any material (type: material) of that rarity; the player chooses the material, and a ×N cost may mix several kinds of material of that rarity. The sources are boss materials and monster materials from higher zones (material rarity per zone: Tiers per zone).
    • The upgrade multiplier applies only to base_atk (weapons) and base_def (armor). crit_bonus and resists do not change.
    • Evolution: only at +5 and only if evolves_to exists. The result is the evolves_to item at +0, with the rarity from that item's data. If the item is currently equipped, the evolved result stays equipped.
    • Evolution uses the item's evolve_cost materials (WeaponData); the EVOLVE button is disabled when materials are missing. The materials in the upgrade table are not used for evolution.
    • "Element stone" in the upgrade table = any element stone (type: element_stone); the player chooses, and a ×N cost may mix kinds of stone. A stone named in evolve_cost must match by ID.
    • Data authors must make the rarity of evolves_to exactly one tier higher. Legendary items must not have evolves_to.
    • "Higher-rarity material" at +4 and +5 = material with a rarity exactly one tier above the item's rarity (Common → Rare, Rare → Epic, Epic → Legendary), not just any higher rarity. For Legendary items, the material is Legendary material.
    • The upgrade cost is taken from the stack chosen in each cost slot on Upgrade screen (it stores the stack uid; an exception to the smallest-stack rule in Order, Quick Slots, and Discarding). If the filling stack shrinks so that it is not enough for all the slots that use it, slots are emptied starting from the rightmost one and then auto-filled again; the evolution cost follows the same rule.
    • No station is needed: upgrade and evolution can be done anywhere through the menu (Stations).
    • Screen: Upgrade screen.
