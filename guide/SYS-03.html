    Survival

    SYS-03

    Summary

    Hunger, Stamina, the day/night cycle, and Heat Zones pressure the player to hunt and cook.

    Rules

    Out of Combat

    • The player is out of combat when they have no target and no monster is chasing or attacking them (Chase or Attack state, Monster Behavior).
    • This definition is the single source; it is used for natural HP recovery (below) and for the automatic opening of Level Up (delay: Formulas & Numbers; display: HUD special states).

    Natural HP Recovery

    • HP recovers on its own when the player has been out of combat for the waiting time and Hunger is above the threshold (numbers: Formulas & Numbers).
    • Recovery stops immediately when the player returns to combat or Hunger drops to or below the threshold; the waiting time restarts from zero.
    • Recovery does not apply while the player is taking Heat Zone damage (resist_fire below the threshold, see Heat Zone below).
    • The recovery rate is 3% of max HP per second, starting after 2 seconds out of combat, as long as Hunger > 25.

    Stamina

    • Stamina is used for running, dodging, and gathering materials (costs: Player, Formulas & Numbers).
    • Stamina recovers while standing still or walking; it does not recover while running, dodging, or gathering materials (Gathering Materials).

    Heat Zone (lava)

    • An area with the heat_zone tag.
    • If resist_fire < 30%: a "Heat" warning appears on the HUD and HP drops.
    • If resist_fire ≥ 30%: the warning still appears, dimmed, and HP does not drop.
    • Warning display: HUD.
