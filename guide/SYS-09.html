    Stations

    SYS-09

    Summary

    The Campfire and the Rune Anvil are world objects. Crafting, upgrading, and cooking do not need a station: the Campfire remains the respawn point, and both stations are shortcuts for opening their screens.

    Rules

    • A station can already exist on the map, or the player can place one using Campfire Kit through Place Mode screen. The Rune Anvil kit was removed; a Rune Anvil already placed in an old save remains and works like a built-in map Rune Anvil.
    • Crafter's Sigil: the wanderer can craft, upgrade, and cook anywhere. On New Game, once the world is ready, the Inscription toast sys.sigil_granted appears once ("[INSCRIPTION] The Crafter's Sigil glows in your palm. You can now craft and cook anywhere.").
    • A placement location is valid when: the tile is in the zone where the player is (current_zone; a tile in another zone is invalid, sys.place_invalid_zone), the tile has no collision, it is not occupied by a gather node or another station, it is not a hazard tile (heat_zone, deep water), it is not inside a boss arena, it is not in the Throne Room (the inner part of the Hollow Palace) before the Palace Gate opens, and it is within 96 px of the player (Euclidean, from the player's center to the tile's center, inclusive). Ground items and monsters do not block a tile.
    • The reason a tile is invalid is shown as a toast: sys.place_invalid_blocked (collision, gather node, or another station), sys.place_invalid_hazard, sys.place_invalid_arena, sys.place_invalid_core_locked, sys.place_invalid_too_far, sys.place_invalid_zone (Place Mode interactions).
    • If a tile fails more than one condition, the toast shows the first reason by priority: another zone (sys.place_invalid_zone) > too far (sys.place_invalid_too_far) > Throne Room sealed (sys.place_invalid_core_locked) > arena (sys.place_invalid_arena) > hazard (sys.place_invalid_hazard) > blocked (sys.place_invalid_blocked).
    • The "nearest valid tile" (Place Mode interactions) uses the same distance metric (player's center to tile's center, Euclidean); on a tie, the smallest y is chosen, then the smallest x.
    • At most 10 player-placed stations per zone (the Station Limit), counted as the total of placed Campfires and placed Rune Anvils (a placed Rune Anvil exists only from old saves), not counting built-in map stations. The zone counted is current_zone. The limit is checked when the player uses the placement tool (Bag screen) for current_zone: if it is full, the toast sys.station_limit "Station limit for this zone reached." appears, Place Mode does not open, and the tool is not consumed. If, when the tool is used, there is no valid tile within range, the toast sys.place_invalid_blocked appears, Place Mode does not open, and the tool is not consumed. The placement zone is restricted to current_zone.
    • A placed station has collision, is permanent (it cannot be dismantled), and works the same as a built-in map station.
    • Tapping a station in the world: the player walks to the station and its screen opens immediately (a shortcut; the same screen can also be opened from the menu anywhere). Rune Anvil → Crafting screen (Upgrade through the navigation tab), Campfire → Cooking screen.
    • There is no read mode and no range requirement (this replaces the old rule): the CRAFT/COOK/UPGRADE/EVOLVE action buttons are never disabled because of the distance to a station. The status texts "Needs a Rune Anvil" and "Needs a campfire" were removed. near_campfire / near_anvil are no longer used by the screens.
    • Crafting screen opened from a Rune Anvil selects the Weapon category, or the last category opened in that play session.

    Formulas & Numbers

    StationNeeded forActive distance
    CampfireSets respawn_point (Death & Respawn); shortcut to Cooking screen64 px, edge-to-edge between collisions (player and station)
    Rune AnvilShortcut to Crafting screen64 px, edge-to-edge between collisions (player and station)

    The collision radius for edge-to-edge distance = ⅓ of the object's body size width, the same as Creatures: a 32×32 station → ≈ 10.7 px, a 48 px player body size → 16 px. Edge-to-edge distance = center distance minus both radii; a value ≤ 64 px means within range, with no rounding.
