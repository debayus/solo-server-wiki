    Elements

    SYS-02

    Summary

    Seven elements with a strong/weak cycle that determines the damage multiplier and monster weaknesses.

    Rules

    Five natural elements form a cycle. Light and Dark are strong against each other.

    Fire ► Wind ► Earth ► Lightning ► Water ► Fire
Light ◄──► Dark

    Meaning of A ► B: an attack from A against B is strong. The damage multiplier and is_weak always come from the element table; weaknesses is for display only and must match the cycle (the converter warns when they differ).

    Element colors and icons: Element colors.

    Formulas & Numbers

    RelationshipDamage multiplier
    Strong×1.5
    Neutral×1.0
    Weak (opposite of the arrow direction)×0.75
    Light ↔ Dark×1.5 both ways
    Element none (attacker or target)×1.0
