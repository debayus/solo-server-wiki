    Zones, Hazards & Ending

    SYS-16

    Summary

    Zone borders and their warnings, each zone's environmental hazards, the Palace Key Shards that open the Throne Room in the Hollow Palace, and the "Go Home" ending. List of zones: Zones.

    Rules

    Entering a Zone

    • Zone borders are drawn by hand on the map. When the player enters a new zone: the toast [INSCRIPTION] Entering: <zone name> (Lv a–b).
    • The same toast also appears when a session starts, for the zone the player is in (the starting zone). That zone is already in visited_zones, so it has no New zone: prefix.
    • If the player's level is below the zone's lower bound, the toast is colored warning with the extra text . Hazard: level too low.
    • A zone entered for the first time is recorded in visited_zones; the first-time zone entry toast gets the prefix New zone: .
    • Text format: [the prefix New zone: if first time] + Entering: <name> (Lv a–b) + [if the level is below the lower bound: . Hazard: level too low]. The text lives in strings_ui.csv, with keys prefixed sys.zone: one for the entry text, one for the hazard variant (it already includes the suffix), and one for the new zone prefix.

    Sight hazards (darkness and glare) do not apply inside a boss arena; the arena is always fully visible.

    Lightning strike damage uses the player's resist like a monster attack (Damage), without DEF.

    Palace Key Shards

    • The six key shards of the Hollow Palace, entrusted by the king to the guardians of each land, one per Z1–Z6 boss, dropped on the First Kill of that boss (Boss).
    • Item type key: cannot be discarded, does not stack, takes no Bag slot (kept in held_keys).
    • Tapping the Palace Gate with all six shards: the shards are set in (removed from held_keys), the gate opens permanently, toast [INSCRIPTION] All six shards unite. The Palace Gate opens..
    • Tapping the Palace Gate without the full set of shards: toast [INSCRIPTION] The gate is sealed. Shards: n/6..

    Ending

    • Every drop from the fight against the final boss while game_cleared = false (including the bonus_drops of body parts, also from failed attempts) goes straight into the Bag before the ending starts (the Bag is unlimited; Monster Death). Later kills in the post-game use the regular drop rules.
    • Only the First Kill of the final boss (while game_cleared is still false) starts the ending; later kills in the post-game do not.
    • Screen: Ending screen. Title Screen: Title screen.
    • Defeating the final boss (the Cursed King) starts an ending sequence made of Inscription text only (not a cutscene, per Out of scope): the screen fades, Inscription notes appear line by line (the curse lifts, the Way Home opens), then the credits roll.
    • When the ending starts: game_cleared = true, then autosave (Autosave).
    • At the end, the player chooses:
      • Go Home: return to the Title Screen.
      • Stay: keep playing (the post-game) from the Throne Room Campfire (Zone 7 map). respawn_point is set automatically to the Throne Room Campfire respawn point (Zone 7 map) without needing to tap it (an exception to the update rule in Death & Respawn).
    • Post-game: every boss can still be fought again (Boss); the Codex shows its Completion percentage.
    • Choosing Go Home and then loading the save: the game continues as the post-game.
