    Discovery & Codex

    SYS-12

    Summary

    Recipes and monster info unlock automatically when the player discovers them; everything is recorded in the Codex.

    Rules

    Recipe Discovery

    Applies to crafting recipes (including Potion recipes) and food recipes. A material counts as "obtained" if it is in codex_items, that is, it has entered the Bag at least once (Materials). An Element Stone (slot 3) counts as a material for the Hint and Ingredients Known stages; a recipe with stone: null has no slot 3 material.

    • The Hint and Ingredients Known stages are computed from codex_items and do not need to be stored.

    Unlocking Monster Info

    InfoUnlocked when
    Name, art, element, level, habitatThe monster is seen for the first time (see below)
    WeaknessThe player has dealt "strong" damage (Elements) to that monster, OR kills ≥ 5
    Specific dropThat item has been obtained from that monster, meaning it came out when the drop was rolled as that monster died or its part broke (Monster Death), even if not yet picked up; recorded in seen_drops (the base chance is shown after kills ≥ 10, plus the moon icon "×1.2 at night" from Formulas & Numbers)
    Body partThe name is visible from the start; the drop bonus is shown after it has been broken

    A monster is "seen" when that monster's node first enters the camera's screen area and is not hidden by a sight hazard (Environmental Hazards), or when the player attacks or is attacked by that monster. At that moment a CodexMonsterEntry is created; the existence of the entry means the monster has been seen.

    Keys

    The Keys tab in the Codex shows 6 Palace Key Shard slots (bosses of Z1–Z6). A slot is filled when that boss is in boss_first_kills, together with the boss name and its zone; an empty slot shows ??? and the zone name if that zone is in visited_zones. The status below the slots: "Palace Gate sealed (n/6)", or "Palace Gate open" after gate_opened.

    Completion

    Codex Completion percentage = (monsters ever seen + materials and stones in codex_items + recipes at the Unlocked stage) ÷ (total monsters + total materials and stones + total crafting and food recipes) × 100, rounded down. Shown on Codex screen and Ending screen.

    Materials

    Materials and Element Stones enter codex_items the first time they enter the Bag (picked up, gathered, crafted, or received as a reward). A drop that has not been picked up is only recorded in the monster's seen_drops (Unlocking Monster Info), not yet in codex_items.

    Item Sources

    The "Found in" list of an item shows only sources that are already known:

    • monsters whose seen_drops contains that item;
    • gather nodes (Gathering Materials) whose yields contains that item, if their zone is in visited_zones.
