    Cooking

    SYS-05

    Summary

    Two or three materials are cooked (anywhere) into food that restores Hunger and grants a buff.

    Rules

    • Slots 1–2 are required; slot 3 is optional (slot 3 without slot 2 is invalid). MATERIAL items only.
    • One item ID can fill only one cooking slot; the picker does not show items that are already in another slot.
    • Matching is order-independent across slots. The set of ingredients must match the recipe exactly (no other ingredients allowed), and the amount of each ingredient must be at least what the recipe needs. If the set of ingredients equals a recipe but some amount is short, cooking is blocked ("Missing materials") and the result is not Burnt Food.
    • When the player chooses an item for a slot, the slot holds the total amount of that ID in the Bag (all stacks); cooking only uses the amount the selected recipe asks for.
    • Tapping a recipe in the list (Cooking screen) fills each slot with the exact amount that recipe needs, and matching uses the amounts in the slots. A manual choice through the picker (trial and error) still puts in the whole stack, and then the matching recipe is selected (the set of ingredients must match exactly; if more than one recipe matches, the one with the largest total ingredient amount is chosen, and if there is still a tie, the first recipe in catalog order, Catalog order).
    • If slots were filled by tapping a recipe and some slot is short, COOK is disabled with the text "Missing materials"; the combination is not matched to another recipe until the player changes the slots manually.
    • Two food recipes must not have an identical set of ingredients and amounts.
    • A set of ingredients that matches no recipe results in Burnt Food (Burnt Food). A set that matches a recipe but has a short amount does not count (see above).
    • Burnt Food uses 1 item from each filled slot.
    • The result of cooking is always 1 item with upgrade_level 0.
    • No Campfire is needed: cooking can be done anywhere through the menu (Stations). The result always goes into the Bag (Stacking). The Campfire still determines the respawn_point: Death & Respawn.
    • Discovering food recipes: Recipe Discovery.
    • Buffs with the same id do not stack: eating again resets the timer.
    • Buff duration uses the INT formula in Derived Stats.
    • Recipe data: the zone catalogs (CNT-Z1 … CNT-Z7). Screen: Cooking screen.

    Eating (Outside Cooking)

    • Eating (from the Bag or a Quick Slot, Order, Quick Slots, and Discarding) consumes 1 FoodData item: Hunger increases by hunger_restore and HP increases by hp_restore (FoodData), and the buff is applied as described above.
    • Allowed when Hunger is 100: Hunger is clamped to 100 and HP to max HP, but the item is still consumed. There is no cooldown between meals.
    • Order of effects when eating: the item is reduced by 1 (smallest stack), the buff is applied, then Hunger increases and HP increases by hp_restore, clamped to max HP after the buff. Food with an hp_max buff can therefore fill HP up to the new max HP.
    • Buffs count down: remaining_sec decreases in real time while the game is not paused. A buff is removed when remaining_sec ≤ 0, then derived stats are recalculated (stats_changed) and HP is clamped if max HP dropped; several buffs that expire in the same step trigger only one stats_changed.
