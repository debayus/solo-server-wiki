    Death & Respawn

    SYS-08

    Summary

    Dying does not remove items; the player comes back to life at the last Campfire.

    Rules

    • Happens when hp ≤ 0. The full sequence: Death Sequence.
    • respawn_point is updated every time the player taps a Campfire in the world, with the player's position when arriving within active distance (Formulas & Numbers) and the Cooking screen open, together with the notification sys.respawn_saved ([INSCRIPTION] This campfire will revive you.). Cooking is not required.
    • Exception to the update rule: choosing "Stay" at the end of the automatic ending sets respawn_point to the Throne Room Campfire respawn point (Zone 7 map, Ending).
    • Respawn happens at respawn_point. Initial value: the Z1 starting point (Zone 1 map, Initial state).
    • After respawn: full HP, full Stamina, Hunger = 50 (even if it was higher before), and all buffs are removed. Reset order: Death Sequence.
    • No items, XP, or levels are lost.
    • Autosave does not happen while the player is dead; player_respawned only clears player_dead and does not save (Autosave).

    Death Sequence

    1. hp ≤ 0: player_died is emitted. The player is immediately invulnerable and input is locked.
    2. Fade out (death_fade_out): the screen fades to black. Monsters that are in Chase or Attack release their target and go to Return (Monster Behavior). A boss in its arena also goes to Return and recovers; boss_fight_active becomes false (Autosave).
    3. Hold (death_hold): black screen, with the Inscription text sys.disconnected ("[INSCRIPTION] Your body collapses... the campfire calls you back.").
    4. Teleport + reset (exactly at the end of the hold, while the screen is still black): the player is moved to respawn_point. Order of the value reset:
      1. remove all buffs;
      2. recalculate derived stats (Derived Stats);
      3. HP = the new max HP, Stamina = the new max Stamina, Hunger = 50;
      4. stats_changed is emitted once.
    5. player_respawned is emitted; player_dead is cleared (without saving).
    6. Fade in (death_fade_in): the screen becomes visible again. Invulnerability and the input lock end exactly when the fade in finishes; there is no extra invulnerability after respawn.

    The durations of the three phases are the tokens death_fade_out, death_hold, and death_fade_in in Alpha and duration tokens (the numbers exist only there).

    Rules during the sequence (from player_died until the fade in finishes):

    • The player is invulnerable to all damage: monsters, Hunger (HP drops at Hunger 0), heat, and lightning (Survival, Environmental Hazards). Player input is ignored.
    • The game clock and spawn timers keep running (they are not paused); the duration of the sequence uses real time.
    • The Death Sequence uses no glitch effect (removed); the fade always plays, even when reduce_effects is on (Effects).
    • The HUD is hidden during the sequence and appears again after player_respawned (HUD).

    Respawn into another zone: if respawn_point is in a different zone from where the player died, the respawn is treated like a normal zone entry (Entering a Zone): that zone is loaded, the zone entry toast appears, the zone is recorded in visited_zones, and the entered_zone autosave is attempted (Autosave). The zone entry signal is emitted after player_respawned so the autosave is not held back; the mechanism is in Scene flow.

    Screen: HUD (HUD). A Campfire placed through Place Mode (Place Mode interactions) works the same as a built-in map Campfire, including updating respawn_point when tapped (Rules).
