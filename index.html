    Hushwood Wiki

    One-line summary

    Through a rift, the player of Hushwood is hurled into the Silent Realm (isekai), an old kingdom whose people were erased by a curse. With no NPCs, they must survive by hunting monsters, crafting weapons and armor, and cooking, then search for a way home.

    Story premise

    • The Silent Realm was once a kingdom that lived on Mana, the blue-green light that flows through soil, wood, and crystal. Its king wanted to live forever and used Mana in forbidden ways. The result was the Silent Curse: every inhabitant vanished in a single night. All that remains is wild nature, ruins, and creatures twisted by the curse (cracks and purple light on their bodies).
    • The player, a Wanderer, is dragged into this world through the same rift. They arrive in the Forest, in front of an old stone tablet.
    • There are no NPCs. No shops, quest givers, or dialogue. The vanished inhabitants are the reason this world is empty.
    • The only "voice" is the Inscription: an ancient tablet left behind by the kingdom, its words appearing as glowing script (notification text prefixed with [INSCRIPTION]). Its style is flat and brief, like carved notes. The Inscription is the sole narrator.
    • The story is told only through Inscription text and item descriptions, not cutscenes (Out of scope).
